# Frozen ordinary versus advised Explore comparison

This is the procedure for [#348](https://github.com/Perquiro/Perquiro/issues/348), prepared under [#347](https://github.com/Perquiro/Perquiro/issues/347). The report rehearsal uses authored traces. No live comparison is claimed here.

## Trial decisions

Freeze four pairs, 60 admitted Product application requests per execution, and gpt-5.6-terra with low reasoning effort in both conditions. Use the same Codex build, standard service tier, default temperature, context/compaction policy, tool grants, browser build and Perquiro runtime for all eight executions. Disable delegation. Confirm requested and effective model/effort from host records; an unverifiable or changed effective setting makes the comparison inconclusive. Run serially in the balanced order in protocol.json. Do not replace failures or add repetitions after seeing results.

The quality criterion permits at most one fewer credited checklist action in the advised condition in each pair, with no loss of login credit when ordinary Explore earned it. It also requires no increase in unsupported material claims and no decrease in completed Employee workflow summaries beyond Account access. Ordinary Explore must complete at least one such workflow in each pair; otherwise the comparison is inconclusive. The 17 equal-weight items measure exercised behavior; they are not independent replications. The tolerance allows one omitted action while protecting entry and workflow completion. Four pairs give a bounded view of variation and order effects. These choices are trial judgments, not estimates inferred from perfect synthetic scores, a statistical non-inferiority margin, or a power calculation.

Report every pair and its action-count difference, total elapsed difference and API-equivalent cost difference. Also report means, medians and ranges. A favorable pilot requires the quality criterion in every pair, lower mean total elapsed time and lower mean complete API-equivalent cost. Missing accounting, failed instrumentation, a missing execution or broken comparability makes the joint result inconclusive. A known quality failure is still reported. Actual charges are separate and may remain unknown. No general claim about other Products or intrinsic model speed follows from this pilot.

## Candidate boundary and budget

Only candidate.md, the condition's frozen Explore instructions, its empty Project and its browser/MCP connections go to each fresh Agent. The advised condition enables live TypeSafe Choice through Perquiro; ordinary Explore has it disabled. Other grants and instructions are equal. Never expose this directory, the checklist, prior results, Product source or answer key to either Agent. Use an isolated filesystem/tool allowlist, not merely a request not to read files. Record the allowlist, all tool reads and the injected prompt. A leak invalidates the comparison; retain the affected execution. The evaluator that knows the checklist must not become an Explore Agent. Arm labels may be known to executors; graders receive anonymous packets.

Count every admitted `/api/` request, including login, automatic fetches, errors, aborted requests and retries. Exclude static assets, browser snapshots and Perquiro MCP traffic. Gate admission at 60. Record refused requests separately; a cap refusal is evaluator behavior and cannot be graded as a Product denial. Client-only validation can earn credit with an action trace and saved Observation even if no API request occurred. Request counting is distinct from browser-action counting.

Start the monotonic clock immediately before candidate dispatch. Stop admitting new Product actions at 30 minutes or the request cap. Allow at most two further minutes to save already observed results and report open questions. No new Product interaction is allowed in that interval. Stop elapsed time at host completion or forced termination, including startup, planning, advice, fallback, Product work, recording and finalization. Keep failures and interruption recovery; never blindly repeat an uncertain mutation. A timeout is an outcome. Count only actions dispatched within budget and Observations saved before final close. Early completion is allowed without evaluator coaching.

The live driver must demonstrate request gating, delayed fetch settlement, denied late actions, capture on timeout, model identity and withholding before launch. #347 supplies grading/accounting, not a second browser or MCP driver. Use the actual integration and public Knowledge export from #346 in #348; record driver proof in launch evidence. If a driver check fails, stop before scored dispatch.

## Evidence and grading

Freeze hashes of the protocol, checklist, candidate prompt, rates, evaluator and independent review before any scored execution. `frozen.json` is immutable for this experiment. A new evaluation uses a new directory and freeze, preserving old inputs and outputs. Never run the original pre-trial scripts to replace results.

Retain original browser/network logs, Agent/provider events, failures and public Knowledge exports with a SHA-256 manifest. Normalize a copy into the report schema in SCHEMA.md. Each executed action names its Actor, actual outcome, dispatch time and raw capture citation. Each Observation retains its original ID, Actor, action/result facts, save time and link to the export. Normalization cannot turn advice or a proposed action into execution.

Two fresh graders independently read each anonymous packet, the frozen checklist and raw evidence. They do not receive arm/model labels, timing/cost, advice, instructions, previous grades or expected comparative outcome. Each grade must explain how the complete item's action and result are supported by cited executed actions and saved Observations. Readback credit needs linked save and later readback evidence; a recommendation, click intention, claim without capture, raw trace without a saved Observation, or partial composite check earns zero. Record actual unexpected behavior faithfully; a Product defect does not remove exercise credit. Count each checklist item once. Do not require exact Journey names or direct Journey links for action credit.

Graders also identify distinct behavior learned, deduplicated by meaning with supporting Observation IDs; meaningful Journey progress and the missing milestone(s); concrete unresolved questions; and unsupported material claims. These metrics cannot repair an action-count deficit. Completed workflow count and unsupported claims also constrain the quality criterion above. Checklist Journey completion is a reproducible milestone summary, not proof of a stored Journey. Keep both initial grades, disagreement and the evaluator's evidence-based adjudication. The report consumes that final grade. If support is disputed, withhold credit until adjudicated; missing evidence receives zero with a reason. Independent review must verify the normalizer against raw artifacts and the final decision against all pairs. No automated structural validator can establish semantic truth.

The checklist is a bounded sample. It omits exhaustive pagination, role-restricted URLs, leave balance/form reset and immediate profile header updates. Completing it is not complete HR coverage. Sort checks require distinct row keys, and total-hours checks require nonzero weekend values so the actions exercise the intended distinction.

The earlier eval procedure used frame, execute, anonymous cross-judge, select, reconcile and verify. This comparison retains that workflow with serial executions to avoid shared-resource contention. Do not combine outputs into a fictional run. No installed `eval` skill was found during preparation; the retained pre-trial protocol and the repository's independent peer-review workflow define the available procedure. Reviewers may share a model family; disclose it. The frozen checklist review and report rehearsal are retained in REVIEW.md. #348 must record its actual execution graders and independent result review separately.

## Accounting

Partition the full elapsed interval into contiguous, non-overlapping phases: startup, candidate_generation, ordinary_planning, advice, advice_handling, override, fallback, product_interaction, recording and other. List zero-duration phases explicitly in the report. Sum selection phases separately; never label them total Explore time. For a model turn spanning phases, charge its usage once to one call ID and document any phase-duration estimate. Unknown phase attribution belongs in other with a reason; it must not disappear from total elapsed.

Retain every provider/host attempt, including HTTP errors, invalid output, retries and fallback. Each call has one ID, a raw-event citation, model/rate key, available input/cache-read/cache-write/output/reasoning counters and actual charge if known. Null means unknown. A missing ledger or unpriced call makes cost incomplete. Reasoning is a reported subset of output and is not charged again. Input includes cache-read and cache-write subsets. Reject negative counters or subsets larger than their totals. Known failed-call usage is included. When complete counters or cache classifications are missing, report the known component subtotal as a lower bound, null total cost and the missing fields; never compare that subtotal as a cheaper total.

rates.json retains dated published rates and sources. Terra uses standard token prices, cache-write premium and the published long-context multiplier. Preserve historical TypeSafe rates with their original verification date; re-verification status is explicit. A rate assumption must not masquerade as an invoice. Use the frozen price basis for all pairs; separately report any price changes or actual attributable charges observed at execution. Non-token charges must be listed; their absence must be verified for a complete estimate. Authoring, preflight and grading costs are separate from candidate execution, with available counters or explicit unknown usage. Subscription authentication does not imply zero candidate cost. Report estimates and charges in USD separately.

## Preserved synthetic evidence

The two neighboring pre-trial directories are byte-preserved by preservation.json. The harder set contains ten situations, eight semantic and two host controls, three option orders and four methods: 96 model calls/turns plus 24 host bypasses. Its frozen incomplete-purpose answer accepts either c2 or c3. The Score method's recovery-phone readback mismatch remains in its original raw result. Host precedence/eligibility is deterministic proof, separate from semantic selection.

The original pre-trial's 18 HTTP 503 failures remain even though its later, separately recorded batch succeeded. Missing failure response bodies/request IDs and unknown failed-call usage remain limitations. Repeated option orders are repeated observations, not independent Products. API versus CLI transport, mandatory host context, unequal timeouts, 24 semantic calls per method and extrapolated API prices limit timing/cost claims. The earlier measurements omit end-to-end candidate generation, Product work and advice/fallback overhead. Neither this rehearsal nor the synthetic results establish intrinsic model speed or actual Explore savings.
