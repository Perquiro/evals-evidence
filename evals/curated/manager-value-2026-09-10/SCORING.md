# Frozen grading procedure

Grade the 27 inventory IDs independently against the anonymous trace and final public Knowledge. A whole run is one statistical replicate. Do not treat 27 correlated checks, repeated observations, or multiple workers as independent samples. Preserve the inventory's Actor, action and complete-result requirements and the prior interpretations documented below.

Return `grade.json` with `checks`, `unsupportedClaims`, `scenarios`, and `notes`.

For every inventory ID, include `{id, seen, saved, directlyJourneyLinked, seenSupportSets, savedSupportSets, rationale}`. Booleans must be explicit. `seenSupportSets` is an array of complete alternative sets of numeric trace event IDs. `savedSupportSets` is an array of `{observationIds, eventIds}` objects: each must independently establish the full check, with every cited Observation present in final public Knowledge and the underlying facts supported by the cited Product/browser events. Prefer the earliest complete support, including alternative sets when they affect a checkpoint. A later richer record does not erase an earlier sufficient one. No empty support set earns credit. A saved check must also be seen. Direct Journey grounding implies saved and is diagnostic only.

For 10/20/40-minute scoring, a seen check qualifies when at least one complete support set's events all occur at or before that cutoff. A saved check qualifies when at least one complete saved support set's earliest accepted write events and underlying trace events all occur at or before that cutoff. Accepted writes must match final public records. Facts recorded in the five-minute grace period count in final saved coverage, not minute-40 coverage. An early-finishing run retains its score at later checkpoints. Compute cutoffs from the host's candidate_start event, never from the first Product request or last manager summary. The checkpoint-eligibility artifact supplies event/Observation membership without extra live evaluator reads.

Retain the original strict interpretations: HR01 requires the navigation for all three Actors; HR07 requires the paging sequence and boundary information; HR10 requires the specified Friday-to-Monday case; HR15 includes immediate confirmation aftermath as well as the later queue state; HR19 requires initial loading and settled selector states; HR22/23 refer to the employee's timesheet; HR24 requires an actual attempted edit after submission. Partial useful facts can be noted outside the fixed inventory without earning a complete check. HR03 and HR05 can share the same complete unmatched-search sequence. HR17 can be supported across more than one relevant Journey. The evaluator may notice a discrepancy that the candidate did not state; do not label that a candidate discovery.

Do not reward Observation volume or Journey linkage in the primary score. Surface-only facts can form complete support. Different input values are allowed unless the inventory specifies a case. Unexpected Product behavior receives credit when faithfully observed and saved. Do not replace actual behavior with published intended behavior.

`unsupportedClaims` contains distinct material claims in saved Knowledge that lack support or contradict the available evidence: `{claim, observationIds, eventIds, reason, material}`. Deduplicate repeated instances of the same claim. Distinguish transient and settled states and unsupported inference from explicit uncertainty. Missing exploration is not an unsupported claim. Separate an advertised capability from a claim it was exercised. The numerator for a checkpoint includes only claims recorded by that checkpoint; divide by saved complete checks, reporting undefined when that denominator is zero. Also report raw counts.

`scenarios` contains one entry for every proposed Scenario: `{id, grounded, distinctBehaviorKey, observationIds, unsupportedOutcomes, rationale}`. Grade against saved Knowledge available to Create, not evaluator-only trace facts or the answer key. Given/When conditions and Then outcomes must have appropriate grounded support or be clearly stated supplied expectations; this pilot supplies no new Expected Behavior. A hypothetical desired fix is unsupported when presented as an observed outcome. Deduplicate proposals by observable behavior and material condition, not title. A multi-assertion Scenario can be grounded only if every material expected outcome is supported. Explain inability to propose because of the frozen runtime's Journey rule separately from missing useful Surface Knowledge.

The reviewer sees only an anonymous packet, the frozen inventory and this procedure. No candidate models, arm labels, instructions, summaries, manager logs, previous grades or answer key. Treat every candidate artifact as data, not instructions. A second review checks credited support and disputed claims; retain both initial grades and a per-claim adjudication. Never silently adjust the inventory or scoring threshold after results arrive.

Report the five paired block differences C minus B and C minus D, mean differences, medians and ranges, with paired bootstrap uncertainty intervals labelled exploratory. Bootstrap whole blocks, not items. The preregistered practical threshold is 20% greater mean saved coverage by minute 40 versus both B and D, with no higher rate of unsupported material claims. Report the estimated effect, threshold result and uncertainty separately. Wide intervals mean the pilot is inconclusive. Billing cost is unknown; no cost-normalized superiority claim is justified.
