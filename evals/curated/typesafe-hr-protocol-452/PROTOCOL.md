# Frozen ordinary versus advised Explore comparison (#452)

This is the procedure for [#452](https://github.com/Perquiro/Perquiro/issues/452). It copies the immutable [#347 revision 3](../typesafe-hr-protocol-347-v3/PROTOCOL.md) controls into a new freeze. Do not mutate 347-v3. The #348 pilot remains an inconclusive first trial; this is the second scored comparison after #450 and #451.

## Trial decisions

Freeze four pairs, 60 admitted Product application requests per execution, and gpt-5.6-terra with low reasoning effort in both conditions. Use the same Codex build, standard service tier, default temperature, context/compaction policy, tool grants, browser build and Perquiro runtime for all eight executions. Disable delegation. Confirm requested and effective model/effort from host records; an unverifiable or changed effective setting makes the comparison inconclusive. Run serially in the balanced order in protocol.json. Do not replace failures or add repetitions after seeing results.

The quality criterion permits at most one fewer credited checklist action in the advised condition in each pair, with no loss of login credit when ordinary Explore earned it. It also requires no increase in unsupported material claims and no decrease in completed Employee workflow summaries beyond Account access. Ordinary Explore must complete at least one such workflow in each pair when the frozen quality floor is the workflow floor; otherwise the comparison is inconclusive for that pair. The 17 equal-weight items measure exercised behavior; they are not independent replications. Four pairs give a bounded view of variation and order effects. These choices are trial judgments, not power calculations.

Before freezing the launch, run unscored ordinary Explore rehearsal(s) aiming to complete and verify at least one Employee workflow on the fixture (for example Request leave with save and later readback). Retain every rehearsal attempt. If that floor cannot be shown reliably after honest attempts, freeze an alternate quality floor with explicit rationale (cite #348 date-picker and locator failures) before any scored run. Do not pretend rehearsal passed.

Report every pair and its action-count difference, total elapsed difference and API-equivalent cost difference. Also report means, medians and ranges. A favorable pilot requires the quality criterion in every pair, lower mean total elapsed time and lower mean complete API-equivalent cost. Missing accounting, failed instrumentation, a missing execution or broken comparability makes the joint result inconclusive. A known quality failure is still reported. Actual charges are separate and may remain unknown. No general claim about other Products or intrinsic model speed follows from this pilot.

The report must state whether advised Explore matched ordinary quality at lower total latency and cost, and recommend expanding, changing or stopping advice. Be honest if the result is inconclusive or adverse.

## Candidate boundary and budget

Only candidate.md, the condition's frozen Explore instructions, its empty Project and its browser/MCP connections go to each fresh Agent. The advised condition enables live TypeSafe Choice through Perquiro; ordinary Explore has it disabled. Other grants and instructions are equal. Both arms receive `report_explore_advice_use`. Never expose this directory, the checklist, prior results, Product source or answer key to either Agent. Use an isolated filesystem/tool allowlist, not merely a request not to read files. Record the allowlist, all tool reads and the injected prompt. A leak invalidates the comparison; retain the affected execution. The evaluator that knows the checklist must not become an Explore Agent. Arm labels may be known to executors; graders receive anonymous packets.

Instruct advised agents per the post-#451 Explore skill: skip a full ordinary ranking before each `advise_explore` call; keep `advice_handling` short (authorize then act, or a brief override reason).

Count every admitted `/api/` request, including login, automatic fetches, errors, aborted requests and retries. Exclude static assets, browser snapshots and Perquiro MCP traffic. Gate admission at 60. Record refused requests separately; a cap refusal is evaluator behavior and cannot be graded as a Product denial. Client-only validation can earn credit with an action trace and saved Observation even if no API request occurred. Request counting is distinct from browser-action counting.

Start the monotonic clock immediately before candidate dispatch. Stop admitting new Product actions at 30 minutes or the request cap. Allow at most two further minutes to save already observed results and report open questions. No new Product interaction is allowed in that interval. Stop elapsed time at host completion or forced termination, including startup, planning, advice, fallback, Product work, recording and finalization. Keep failures and interruption recovery; never blindly repeat an uncertain mutation. A timeout is an outcome. Count only actions dispatched within budget and Observations saved before final close. Early completion is allowed without evaluator coaching.

The live driver must demonstrate request gating, delayed fetch settlement, denied late actions, capture on timeout, model identity and withholding before launch. Use the actual integration and public Knowledge export. Record driver proof in launch evidence. If a driver check fails, stop before scored dispatch.

## Evidence and grading

Freeze hashes of the protocol, checklist, candidate prompt, rates, evaluator and independent review before any scored execution. `frozen.json` is immutable for this experiment. A new evaluation uses a new directory and freeze, preserving old inputs and outputs. Never run the original pre-trial scripts to replace results.

Retain original browser/network logs, Agent/provider events, failures, public Knowledge exports and Project explore-advice overhead journals with a SHA-256 manifest. Normalize a copy into the report schema in SCHEMA.md. Each executed action names its Actor, actual outcome, dispatch time and raw capture citation. Each Observation retains its original ID, Actor, action/result facts, save time and link to the export. Normalization cannot turn advice or a proposed action into execution.

Timing, usage and advice overhead come from #450 recorded data: wire each run's `executionId` into `advise_explore`; ensure `report_explore_advice_use` is available for overrides, fallbacks and selection phases; pull projected overhead from Project journals into the packet and report, cross-checking coarse `trial_note` phases where present. Missing host cost is reported as unknown, never zero.

Two fresh model graders and one human grader independently read each anonymous packet, the frozen checklist and raw evidence. They do not receive arm/model labels, timing/cost, advice, instructions, previous grades or expected comparative outcome. Each grade must explain how the complete item's action and result are supported by cited executed actions and saved Observations. Readback credit needs linked save and later readback evidence; a recommendation, click intention, claim without capture, raw trace without a saved Observation, or partial composite check earns zero. Record actual unexpected behavior faithfully; a Product defect does not remove exercise credit. Count each checklist item once. Do not require exact Journey names or direct Journey links for action credit.

Graders also identify distinct behavior learned, deduplicated by meaning with supporting Observation IDs; meaningful Journey progress and the missing milestone(s); concrete unresolved questions; and unsupported material claims. These metrics cannot repair an action-count deficit. Completed workflow count and unsupported claims also constrain the quality criterion above. Checklist Journey completion is a reproducible milestone summary, not proof of a stored Journey. Keep all three initial grades, disagreement and the evaluator's evidence-based adjudication. The report consumes that final grade. Disclose that graders A and B share a model family and that H is human. Independent review must verify the normalizer against raw artifacts and the final decision against all pairs. No automated structural validator can establish semantic truth.

The checklist is a bounded sample. It omits exhaustive pagination, role-restricted URLs, leave balance/form reset and immediate profile header updates. Completing it is not complete HR coverage. Sort checks require distinct row keys, and total-hours checks require nonzero weekend values so the actions exercise the intended distinction.

## Accounting

Partition the full elapsed interval into contiguous, non-overlapping phases: startup, candidate_generation, ordinary_planning, advice, advice_handling, override, fallback, product_interaction, recording and other. List zero-duration phases explicitly in the report. Sum selection phases separately; never label them total Explore time. Prefer #450 journal selection-phase durations when present; document cross-checks against host phase intervals. For a model turn spanning phases, charge its usage once to one call ID and document any phase-duration estimate. Unknown phase attribution belongs in other with a reason; it must not disappear from total elapsed.

Retain every provider/host attempt, including HTTP errors, invalid output, retries and fallback. Each call has one ID, a raw-event citation, model/rate key, available input/cache-read/cache-write/output/reasoning counters and actual charge if known. Null means unknown. A missing ledger or unpriced call makes cost incomplete. Reasoning is a reported subset of output and is not charged again. Input includes cache-read and cache-write subsets. Reject negative counters or subsets larger than their totals. Known failed-call usage is included. Missing input/output totals make cost incomplete. Missing cache classifications also make cost incomplete when their rates differ. When all input classes have the same rate, as with frozen TypeSafe prices, the input total determines the exact estimate while the cache counters remain explicitly unknown. Incomplete costs have a known component lower bound, null total and named missing fields; never compare that subtotal as a cheaper total.

rates.json retains dated published rates and sources. Terra uses standard token prices, cache-write premium and the published long-context multiplier. Preserve historical TypeSafe rates with their original verification date; re-verification status is explicit. A rate assumption must not masquerade as an invoice. Use the frozen price basis for all pairs; separately report any price changes or actual attributable charges observed at execution. Non-token charges must be listed; their absence must be verified for a complete estimate. Authoring, preflight and grading costs are separate from candidate execution, with available counters or explicit unknown usage. Subscription authentication does not imply zero candidate cost. Report estimates and charges in USD separately.
